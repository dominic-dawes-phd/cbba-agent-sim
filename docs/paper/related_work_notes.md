Read in full Oct 28, 2026 (PDF supplied by the user). CoBA: market-based allocation with AND/OR task trees under temporal constraints (Sec. 4.1) and a dynamic acquaintance network so robots submit "indirect bids" on behalf of teammates in other sub-networks (Sec. 3). It targets the "candidate absent" problem, where a task fails because capable robots never hear the announcement.
  - **Experiments:** USARSim, a 400x400 arena, 20 robots of three types (8 Talon, 8 P2AT, 4 Soryu), Poisson task arrival (lambda = 8), a Gaussian lossy-comm model, 20 runs per setting, and metrics NCT and NFT (completed and failed tasks). Baselines: TraderBots and COBOS.

There is also a physical-robot bar chart against comm distance (20/40/60 m) and tasks outstanding over time, constrained vs perfect comms (Fig. 12).
  - **Findings we cite:** differences are negligible for small teams (Fig. 5), and indirect auctions stop paying off when comms are nearly team-wide (Fig. 7 at reliability 180).
  - **Overlap:** comm-constrained market allocation with task structure; the acquaintance network is a relay idea we have not built.
  - **How ours differs:** no LLM utilities, a static acquaintance model, and no churn sweep.
